Delete Your MISSION Account

Last updated: September 29, 2026

MISSION allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your MISSION account, send an email to:

Account deletion email nathprakash728@gmail.com Request Account Deletion

Please send the request from the email address associated with your MISSION account. Include the email address associated with your account so that we can identify and process the request.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When an account deletion request is processed, MISSION will delete the user's account and associated personal data stored for the operation of the MISSION service.

This may include account information and associated app data such as profile information, goals, assessment results, mission progress, XP, achievements, and other account-associated data, where applicable.

Subscriptions and Purchases

Deleting a MISSION account does not automatically cancel an active subscription purchased through Google Play.

If you have an active subscription, manage or cancel it through Google Play to prevent future subscription charges.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period when required by law or when necessary for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, accounting, or other legitimate legal obligations.

Any information that must be retained will be kept only for as long as necessary for the applicable purpose.

Processing Time

We will review eligible account deletion requests and process them within a reasonable period.

If additional information is required to verify the request, we may contact you using the email address provided.

Important: Account deletion is permanent. Once your account and associated MISSION data have been deleted, they may not be recoverable.

Contact
