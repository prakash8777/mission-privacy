Delete Your MISSION Account

Last updated: September 29, 2026

MISSION allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your MISSION account, send an email to nathprakash728@gmail.com using the email address associated with your MISSION account.

Email subject: MISSION Account Deletion Request

Please include the email address associated with your MISSION account in your request so that we can identify the account and process the deletion.

What data will be deleted

When an account deletion request is processed, MISSION will delete the user's account and associated personal data that is stored for the operation of the MISSION service, including account information and associated app data such as profile, goals, assessment results, mission progress, XP, achievements, and other account-associated data where applicable.

Subscriptions and purchases

Deleting a MISSION account does not automatically cancel an active subscription purchased through Google Play. Users should manage or cancel their subscription through Google Play before requesting account deletion if they do not want future subscription charges.

Data that may be retained

Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period when required by law, for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, accounting, or other legitimate legal obligations. Any retained information will be kept only for as long as necessary for the applicable purpose.

Processing time

We will review account deletion requests and process eligible requests within a reasonable period. If additional information is required to verify the request, we may contact you using the email address provided.

Contact
