Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Overview

MISSION ("MISSION", "we", "us", or "our") is an AI-powered career and skill-development application that helps users turn goals into personalized learning missions, evaluate progress, and build skills through practical challenges.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when it may be shared, and the choices available to you when you use the MISSION mobile application and related services.

MISSION is intended for users who are old enough to use the service under the laws applicable to them. MISSION is not directed to children under 13.

2. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account, we may collect your email address, authentication information, and the display name you choose to use in the app.

Goals and learning information

MISSION stores information you provide about your learning or career goals, including goal titles, descriptions, goal type, target dates, and progress.

Skill and assessment information

We may collect assessment responses, confidence selections, skill scores, proficiency levels, strengths, focus areas, mission history, achievements, XP, levels, streaks, and related learning-progress information.

Mission answers and evaluations

When you complete a mission, we collect the answer or other response you submit. This information may be processed to evaluate your response, generate feedback, update skill progress, and recommend subsequent missions.

Subscription and purchase information

If you purchase MISSION Pro, purchase and subscription information is processed through Google Play and RevenueCat. We use information provided by these services to determine subscription status and provide the corresponding features. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number through the MISSION app.

Device and technical information

We may receive limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service, such as app version, operating system information, device-related identifiers, network information, and error or diagnostic information, where provided by the platforms or services we use.

3. How We Use Information

  • Create and maintain your MISSION account.
  • Save your goals, assessments, missions, and learning progress.
  • Generate personalized missions and recommendations.
  • Evaluate mission responses and provide AI-assisted feedback.
  • Calculate skill progression, XP, levels, streaks, and achievements.
  • Provide and manage MISSION Pro subscriptions.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and security incidents.
  • Diagnose errors and improve app reliability and performance.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. AI Processing

MISSION uses artificial intelligence to help generate missions, evaluate submitted responses, identify skill gaps, and personalize learning recommendations.

Information needed for these functions, which may include your goal, skill information, mission context, and submitted answer, may be sent to third-party AI service providers acting as processors or service providers for MISSION.

We use technical controls intended to limit the information sent to AI services to what is reasonably necessary for the requested AI function. You should avoid submitting passwords, payment-card numbers, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information in mission answers.

5. Service Providers and Third Parties

We use selected third-party services to operate MISSION. Depending on the features you use, these may include:

  • Supabase — authentication, database storage, and backend services.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management and entitlement status.
  • Google Play — app distribution and payment processing for Google Play purchases.
  • AI service providers — AI generation and evaluation used by MISSION.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual obligations. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Data Storage and Security

MISSION uses cloud services to store account and learning information. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential and for notifying us if you believe your account has been compromised.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain your account, provide your learning history, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, comply with legal obligations, and protect our legitimate business interests.

When information is no longer required, we may delete it, anonymize it, or retain it only where legally permitted or required.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, and to request a copy of certain information.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the contact address listed below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

9. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your MISSION account and associated personal information by using the account-deletion option provided in the MISSION app when available, or by contacting us at the privacy contact address below.

Some information may be retained when necessary to comply with law, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, or maintain legitimate business records.

10. Children's Privacy

MISSION is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

If a higher minimum age applies in your jurisdiction, that local minimum age applies.

11. International Processing

MISSION and its service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country in which you live. Where required, we take appropriate measures for international transfers and processing in accordance with applicable law.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as MISSION, our services, or applicable legal requirements change. We will update the "Last updated" date when changes are made. If a change is material, we may provide additional notice where appropriate.
